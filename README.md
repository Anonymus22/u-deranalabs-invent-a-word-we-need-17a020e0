## The entry

**Roomnesia** — forgetting why you entered a room the moment you step through the doorway.

## Why this word fills a real gap

Everyone has lived it: you stand up with a clear purpose, walk through a doorway, and the reason evaporates the instant you cross the threshold. Psychologists call the phenomenon the "doorway effect", but English has no single word for the experience itself — you have to describe it in a whole sentence. *Roomnesia* (room + amnesia) names it in one breath: it is sayable, spellable, instantly understandable, and it turns a small daily bewilderment into something you can laugh about with a friend instead of just shrugging at.
